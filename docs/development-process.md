# Development Process

Torizon is maintained by the Toradex R&D team. Development happens in the
`meta-torizon` and `meta-torizon-bsp` repositories, including issues, PRs, and
discussions. These repositories are then used by our internal CI/CD
infrastructure.

We also track issues, bugs and features internally. Because of this, some of the
commits and pull requests made by Toradex team members may contain references to
internal ticket identifiers e.g. 'Related-to: TOR-3705'.
